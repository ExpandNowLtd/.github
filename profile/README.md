# ExpandNow

**Local commerce infrastructure for global expansion.**

ExpandNow enables brands to sell in new markets by providing the local seller infrastructure — compliance, importation, tax, fulfillment and returns — while the brand retains control of its products and customer relationships.

We operate as Merchant of Record and Importer of Record across the UK, EU and US, supporting direct-to-consumer, marketplace and social commerce channels.
